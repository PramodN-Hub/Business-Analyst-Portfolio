# Sales & Business Performance Analytics

## Project Overview

An end-to-end Business Analyst and Data Analytics portfolio project demonstrating business requirements gathering, SQL analysis, Excel/Power Query, Power BI dashboard development, data validation and UAT.

## Business Problem

The organization relies on manually prepared sales reports to monitor business performance. Management needs a centralized solution to analyze revenue, profitability, regional performance, product performance and customer trends.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an interactive analytics solution that helps business stakeholders:

- Monitor sales and profit performance
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Compare regional performance
- Identify top and underperforming products
- Analyze customer segments
- Track key business KPIs
- Make data-driven decisions
